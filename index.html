Giorgio Bono

Data Science and Engineering Student at Politecnico di Torino. Erasmus student at CentraleSupélec and Université de Lorraine.

Master's thesis student at KTH RPL

Stockholm, Sweden

bono.giorgio.02@gmail.com

I am a Master’s student in Data Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Torino (Italy), specializing in artificial intelligence. As part of an Erasmus+ mobility program, I studied at CentraleSupélec within Université Paris-Saclay (France). I am also pursuing a double degree with Université de Lorraine (France) through its IA²VR Master’s program (Intelligence Artificielle et ses Applications en Vision et Robotique).

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino.

I am conducting my Master’s thesis at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, within the Department of Robotics, Perception and Learning (RPL). My research focuses on multi-task reinforcement learning and task representations for robotics.

I contributed to Squadra Corse PoliTO, working on LiDAR-based systems for autonomous driving applications.

Previously, I participated in the UROP@PoliTO research program with the project From Natural Language Text to Source Code. I investigated approaches to improve small and medium-sized language models, up to 15B parameters, for text-to-SQL tasks on the BIRD benchmark, experimenting with AdalFlow for optimization and evaluation.

I worked at Synesthesia as an AI Workshop Instructor for Teachers. I designed and delivered workshops introducing artificial intelligence concepts to elementary school educators and taught practical uses of accessible AI tools, including image generators and chatbots, for classroom integration.
